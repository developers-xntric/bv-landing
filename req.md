Dedicated Google Ads landing pages for PPF, STEK PPF, Car Wrapping, and Car Wrap Price

Purpose
This document gives the recommended content structure for dedicated landing pages. The goal is to improve Google Ads landing page experience by matching each ad group to a clear URL, title, meta description, H1, body copy, FAQs, and form fields.
Recommended URL Mapping
Ad group / intent	Recommended URL	Main keyword theme
PPF Dubai	/ppf-dubai	paint protection film Dubai, car PPF Dubai
STEK	/stek-ppf-dubai	STEK PPF Dubai, STEK DYNOshield
Wrapping Dubai	/car-wrapping-dubai	car wrapping Dubai, vinyl wrap Dubai
Wrapping Price & Cost	/car-wrap-price-dubai	car wrap price Dubai, vehicle wrap cost

Global implementation rules
Use one clear H1 per page matching the main ad group keyword.
Place the primary keyword in the SEO title, meta description, H1, first paragraph, one H2, one FAQ, and the form heading.
Avoid keyword stuffing. Use keyword variations naturally throughout the copy.
Keep the quote form visible above the fold on mobile.
Use compressed WebP images. Hero image under 300 KB and below-fold images lazy-loaded.
Add WhatsApp and Call CTAs above the fold.
Add budget range and timeline fields to improve lead quality.

Technical landing page improvements to support Quality Score
Compress all large images and convert to WebP/AVIF. Keep hero image under 300 KB where possible.
Use fetchpriority="high" on the hero image and do not lazy-load the hero.
Lazy-load all below-the-fold images using loading="lazy" and decoding="async".
Defer non-critical JavaScript and remove duplicate tracking scripts.
Use one primary CTA above the fold on mobile: Get Quote / WhatsApp.
Keep each page focused on one service intent instead of mixing all services equally.
Add internal links between relevant pages: PPF, STEK PPF, Car Wrapping, Car Wrap Price.
